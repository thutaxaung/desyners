Questions

Good to know.

Something else on your mind? Send us a message — a real person answers.

I only have an idea. Is that enough?

Yes. Tell us what you want people to be able to do. We turn it into screens and a plan, and you approve them before we build anything.

What does "AI included" mean for my project?

Each project gets AI features that suit it — for example an assistant that answers users, smart search, reports that write themselves, or reading data from photos and documents. It's part of the build, not an add-on.

Can you build for both Android and iOS?

Yes. We build one app that runs on both, and we publish it to Google Play and the App Store for you.

Do you take on school projects?

Yes — systems for schools, like results, attendance and fees, and student projects too. Tell us what you need and your deadline.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size. Small tools can be ready quickly; bigger systems like an ERP are delivered in stages, so you can start using parts early.

How much will it cost?

Every project is different. Once we understand your idea, we send a clear quote with no surprises.
